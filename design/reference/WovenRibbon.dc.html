Woven Ribbon

A tapered crimson shape — thicker at mid-span, narrowing to a point at each passage — read as a physical ribbon draped across the mountain rather than a drawn line. The most tactile and dimensional of the four, and the easiest to spot at a glance even with several on screen at once. Risk: needs real design polish to avoid looking like a rendering error rather than an intentional shape — the highest-craft option to actually build well.
